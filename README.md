# Social Coworker

**Social Coworker** is an AI-powered social media management platform for creators, freelancers, and small agencies managing multiple brands. The platform generates brand-specific content using artificial intelligence, applies a one-click human approval workflow, and publishes to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and X (Twitter) from a single dashboard.

Website: [socialcoworker.com](https://socialcoworker.com)

---

## What is Social Coworker?

Social Coworker combines AI content generation, multi-brand management, and native social media publishing in a single tool. Each brand operates as an isolated project with its own AI voice, OAuth-secured account connections, and customizable approval flows.

Unlike traditional social media schedulers such as Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later, Social Coworker generates the content itself — tailored to each brand's voice — rather than only scheduling content users have created elsewhere. Unlike generic AI writing tools, Social Coworker publishes directly to the social platforms, removing the need for a separate scheduler.

## Key features

- **Brand-specific AI content generation** — each brand has its own AI voice
- **Multi-brand management** — handle multiple brands or clients from one dashboard
- **One-click human approval** — every post is reviewed before publishing
- **Native publishing** — LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and X via official OAuth
- **AI Reels generation** — short-form video created automatically
- **Post scheduling** — calendar view across all connected platforms
- **Team collaboration** — role-based access per brand or project

## Who is it for?

- Freelance social media managers handling multiple clients
- Founders managing both personal and company brands
- Small marketing agencies maintaining consistent output across accounts
- Creators with several active brands or projects
